The Three Pillars of Maïz

Every dish is an intersection of ancient culinary wisdom, pasture-raised raw ingredients, and precise flame mastery.

01

Prime Cuts & Single Spices

We exclusively grind whole cuts of pasture-raised beef chuck and grass-fed lamb daily. Combined with whole coriander, wild Egyptian sumac, and hand-toasted cumin for authentic aromatics.

02

Stone-Milled Heritage Dough

Our baladi bread is crafted from ancient whole wheat varieties, cold-fermented for 72 hours and baked fresh for every order to achieve a blistered, crackling exterior and tender crumb.

03

The Charcoal Hearth

No gas burners. No shortcuts. Every Hawawshi and skewer is roasted directly over natural oak and citrus wood lump charcoal, delivering unforgettable smokiness and caramelized crust.

Live Fire Gastronomy

Mastery Born in Smoke and Embers

Grilling is not merely cooking—it is an art of heat calibration. The fat renders inside the bread, bathing the spiced filling in rich juices while the flames char the perimeter.

  • Intense Radiance (450°C)

    Flash-sears the outer crust in seconds, locking in rich moisture and forming the iconic crispy blistered bubbles.

  • Slow Fat Clarification

    Grass-fed tallow and cultured clarified ghee slowly render through the dough, yielding unparalleled texture.
